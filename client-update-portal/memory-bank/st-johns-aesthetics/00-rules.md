# SJAWC report editorial rules

- Permanent title: Marketing and Sales Performance Report.
- Permanent hero paragraph: Digital Marketing and sales performance activity using a rolling 30-day window. Sales metrics are a blend from LeadAR and Boulevard matching criteria on email address. Performance tags within LeadAR drive visibility into the digital marketing efforts for the source summaries.
- Executive Summary: write as Michael/Stott Marketing speaking directly to Lorie, professionally friendly, first person, positive and useful. Lead generation, awareness, and new business take priority over sales attribution. Use all relevant verified results; never invent gains or email/SMS campaign results. Do not mention years of experience.
- Present each new or revised Executive Summary to the user BEFORE adding it to the client report. Wait for explicit approval of that text. Never infer approval from a request to update metrics or from a prior summary approval.
- Drafts are stored separately under draft in data/sjawc/executive_summary.json. The renderer uses only approved paragraphs. Only after approval may the approved text, period, approval date and integrity hash be updated. Do not regenerate the approved text automatically during weekly refreshes.
- Scheduled metric refreshes retain the dated, previously approved summary. A new summary requires a fresh review. Never silently rewrite its figures for a new period.
- Keep detailed matching/tracking QA in private artifacts, not client-facing findings cards.
- Preserve gross-sales labels and distinct sales/ad periods. Do not infer Google acquisition from generic appointment tags.
- Clearly distinguish local preview, deployment in progress, and verified production. Say published only after deployment success and production verification.
