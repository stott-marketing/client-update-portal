Executive Summary

Across the Punch Club portfolio, our work is expanding discoverability, improving audience quality and creating measurable demand. During August 8–September 6, 2026, the portfolio generated 8,478 website sessions, 298 organic search clicks, 46 key events and $5,427.12 in GA4-recorded revenue. The strongest signal is sustained progress across search, content, paid media, lead generation and the reporting systems that connect the work.

Organic growth is breaking through

Phil Medeiros reached an average page-one position as clicks increased 125% and impressions increased 1,624%. Punch Transfers grew page-level impressions 82%, established a page-one content asset and moved its core DTF product page forward 14 positions. South Coast Towing and Tony’s Auto also improved their average search positions.

Demand is being created efficiently

Grub Tub generated 50,835 Meta impressions, 3,577 clicks and 1,868 landing-page views at an $0.08 CPC. Modern Auto Body produced five Instant Form leads at $25.98 each. Kathy Mackenzie recorded 764 campaign clicks at a 3.85% CTR, while LC Mechanical increased GA4 key events 60%.

Implementation is strengthening performance

Chem Nut Supply increased sessions 26.2% and active users 24.9%. South Coast Towing increased sessions 8.3% and active users 14.4%. Tony’s Auto raised engagement from 42.0% to 55.3%, and Punch Creatives improved sessions, users, engagement and organic clicks. These gains are supported by broader analytics coverage and cleaner client reporting.

Next strategic priority: Turn the reach we have created into more verified business outcomes. That means completing conversion tracking for calls, forms, store clicks and purchases; advancing high-opportunity pages into top search positions; and following every lead through to an estimate, booking or sale. The portfolio has momentum. Our next standard is clearer attribution and stronger conversion from that demand.
