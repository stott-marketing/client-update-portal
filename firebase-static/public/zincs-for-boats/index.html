    Executive Summary

    For June 1 through June 30, 2026, Shopify recorded 401 orders and $76,794.57 in store revenue. GA4 recorded 6,053 active users, 8,148 sessions, 313 ecommerce purchases, and $33,890.65 in tracked revenue. Year to date through June 30, Shopify shows $480,967.58 in revenue from 2,665 orders, while GA4 shows $304,075.07 in tracked revenue from 2,127 ecommerce purchases. The strongest June channel by tracked revenue was Cross-network, with $12,479.21 from 146 purchases.

    Organic search is meaningful but has clear upside. Search Console recorded 1,064 clicks and 90,743 impressions with an average position of 9.4. Search Atlas shows 726 tracked keywords, 49 top-3 rankings, a site health score of 85, and 29 referring domains. Google Ads API data shows $4,950.43 in spend, $30,994.96 in conversion value, and 6.26x reported ROAS. The key operating focus is improving the channels that already prove purchase intent while using SEO to lower dependency on paid acquisition.
