    Executive Summary

    For June 1 through June 30, 2026, Shopify recorded 385 orders and $74,511.01 in store revenue. GA4 recorded 5,666 active users, 7,439 sessions, 316 ecommerce purchases, and $31,578.48 in tracked revenue. Year to date through June 30, Shopify shows $492,279.35 in revenue from 2,750 orders, while GA4 shows $311,089.89 in tracked revenue from 2,201 ecommerce purchases. The strongest June channel by tracked revenue was Cross-network, with $12,798.77 from 160 purchases.

    Organic search is meaningful but has clear upside. Search Console recorded 1,024 clicks and 87,585 impressions with an average position of 9.5. Search Atlas shows 726 tracked keywords, 49 top-3 rankings, a site health score of 85, and 29 referring domains. Google Ads API data shows $4,561.99 in spend, $31,281.51 in conversion value, and 6.86x reported ROAS. The key operating focus is improving the channels that already prove purchase intent while using SEO to lower dependency on paid acquisition.
