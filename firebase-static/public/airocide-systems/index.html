Lead Reporting Standard

A submitted Contact Us form should be counted as a direct conversion. Commercial-intent visitors who do not submit a form should be reported separately as behavior signals, using page visits, Contact Us page activity, quote/contact CTA clicks, form starts, abandoned sessions, repeat visits, and Clarity recordings. That keeps the report useful without overstating anonymous traffic as named leads.
